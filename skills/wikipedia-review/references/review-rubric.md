# Wikipedia draft review rubric

Score each applicable dimension from 0 to 4 and preserve the evidence behind
the score.

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Missing, fabricated, or unusable |
| 1 | Major defects dominate |
| 2 | Mixed result requiring material revision |
| 3 | Strong and usable with bounded improvement |
| 4 | Excellent and fully supported for the draft's scope |

## Core dimensions

### Source support

Sources are identifiable, appropriate to the claims, sufficiently independent
and deep, and not inflated through syndication or trivial mentions.

### Claim fidelity

The draft says no more than the sources support, preserves uncertainty, avoids
new synthesis, and represents contradictions accurately.

### Citation correctness

Citations identify the right sources, use accurate metadata and locators, sit
next to supported claims, and are reused cleanly.

### Scope and structure

The article has a coherent subject boundary, useful lead, appropriate section
order, sensible proportions, and no padding or missing load-bearing context.

### Neutrality and proportion

Language informs rather than promotes or attacks. Assessments and disputes are
attributed. Coverage weight follows the source landscape.

### Original expression

The draft is genuinely paraphrased, does not mirror source structure too
closely, and uses quotations only where wording matters.

### Prose quality

Writing is clear, specific, cohesive, varied, and free of distracting generic
or templated patterns.

### Format quality

Markdown or wikitext is internally consistent. References, links, templates,
lists, headings, and navigation render or parse as intended.

### Learning value

The draft's sources, claims, reviews, and editorial choices are inspectable
enough to teach from.

### Platform-request fidelity

When applicable, the request identifies the intended outcome, accurately
describes the page or decision at issue, connects its rationale to the evidence
and article changes, and keeps unresolved gaps visible.

## Gold result

A gold draft requires scores of 4 for source support, claim fidelity, and
citation correctness, with every other applicable dimension at 3 or higher. It
also requires no unresolved blocking finding.

Non-gold drafts remain useful. Report the evidence or revision that would move
each weak dimension forward.
