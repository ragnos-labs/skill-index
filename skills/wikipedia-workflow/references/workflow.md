# Wikipedia workflow stages

Use this reference for a full Wiki Lab run.

| Stage | Main skill | Required output | Completion signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Intake | wikipedia-workflow | Scope and run manifest | Topic, page type, depth, and outputs are clear |
| 2. Research | wikipedia-research | Source packet | Sources are normalized and limitations are visible |
| 3. Claims | wikipedia-research | Claim ledger | Important candidate claims map to direct support |
| 4. Design | wikipedia-writing | Outline options and selected structure | Article shape follows the source landscape |
| 5. Draft | wikipedia-writing | Complete article variants | Each draft is complete enough to review |
| 6. Citations and request | wikipedia-writing | Citation records, optional wikitext, and any platform-request draft | Citations support the article and request rationale |
| 7. Review | wikipedia-review | Independent findings and scores | Evidence, article, and format passes are recorded |
| 8. Prose review | ai-writing-review | Pattern findings | Generic patterns and overcorrection risks are visible |
| 9. Revision | wikipedia-writing | Revised article, request, and change notes | Changes preserve support and improve the artifacts |
| 10. Package | wikipedia-workflow | Gold or developmental draft packet | Article, request, and evidence can be inspected together |

## Stage 1: Intake

Record the topic, aliases, page type, drafting purpose, intended reader,
requested depth, supplied sources, existing drafts, desired formats, requested
platform process, and sensitivities.

## Stages 2 and 3: Evidence

Do not move directly from search results to prose. Normalize sources, inspect
their direct coverage, separate independent from affiliated material, detect
duplication, preserve exact locators, and map candidate claims.

## Stage 4: Design

Choose structure based on source emphasis and the subject's development. For a
teaching run, compare alternatives by coverage, clarity, neutrality, and likely
reader questions.

## Stages 5 and 6: Article production

Draft complete articles. Place citations while drafting rather than attaching a
reference list afterward. Generate wikitext only after citation IDs and reuse
are stable enough to avoid mechanical churn. When a platform request is part of
the assignment, draft the complete request from the same evidence and keep it
separate from the article.

## Stages 7 and 8: Independent review

Review against sources, not the drafter's explanation. Keep factual and
citation findings separate from prose-pattern findings so style cannot hide an
evidence problem.

## Stage 9: Revision

Order the revision plan:

1. fabricated, unsupported, or contradicted material;
2. citation identity, placement, and source-use errors;
3. scope, weight, attribution, and structure;
4. close paraphrase;
5. reader clarity and context; and
6. generic or templated prose.

After revision, compare both versions for meaning drift and lost citations.

## Stage 10: Package

A gold packet is more than clean prose. Preserve enough evidence and review
history that another person can reconstruct the important editorial decisions
behind the article and any platform request.
