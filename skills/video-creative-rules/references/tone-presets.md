# Tone presets by workflow

One preset per Video Studio brief workflow. Each sets energy, voice, typography,
pacing, hook, highlight and landing style, and transitions. Presets are defaults;
the brief's creative direction refines or overrides them, and the human's
instruction overrides both.

## interview (Interview / social clip)

- Energy: warm, present, unhurried. The person is the content.
- Voice: theirs. Captions quote, they do not paraphrase.
- Typography: mixed case captions, medium weight, high contrast, safe margins.
- Pacing: cuts follow speech; 3 to 6 second holds; no cut inside a clause.
- Hook: the strongest sentence they said, cold open, no title card first.
- Highlight: one idea per segment; a name or context lower-third once, early.
- Landing: the line that closes the thought, then the handle or where to watch more.
- Transitions: straight cuts; a soft crossfade only across a topic change.

## lesson (Course / lesson)

- Energy: calm, clear, one idea at a time.
- Voice: the instructor's. On-screen text supports, never competes.
- Typography: clean sans, larger than feels necessary, one weight for terms.
- Pacing: 4 to 7 second holds; a term stays until it has been used.
- Hook: the question the segment answers, or the mistake it prevents.
- Highlight: show the thing being taught (screen, diagram, example), label it.
- Landing: the one-sentence takeaway, then what comes next.
- Transitions: clean slide or cut; no motion that reads as decoration.

## announcement (Company / campaign announcement)

- Energy: playful, clean, postable; confident without hype.
- Voice: first person plural. Direct. No corporate filler.
- Typography: display weight for the claim, mixed case, room to breathe.
- Pacing: 4 to 5 scenes of 3 to 5 seconds; 15 to 25 seconds total.
- Hook: a simple observation or the product's own strongest line.
- Highlight: two or three short phrases, one idea per scene, real UI or footage under each.
- Landing: name, tagline, one action. Light, not loud.
- Transitions: crossfade or clean wipe, 0.3 to 0.6s.

## demo (Product / screen demo)

- Energy: smooth, feature-forward, honest about what is shown.
- Voice: feature then benefit, present tense, third person.
- Typography: title case labels, medium weight, never over the UI being shown.
- Pacing: 4 to 6 scenes; each names the screen or feature, then shows it working.
- Hook: the product doing its key action inside the first two seconds.
- Highlight: entry, key action, result, in that order, with a simulated cursor or tap where it helps.
- Landing: where to try it; a URL or a call to action, nothing else.
- Transitions: slide or smooth wipe, 0.35 to 0.45s; no hard cuts mid-flow.

## revision (Client revision / handoff)

- Energy: whatever the original cut had. Match it; do not restyle.
- Voice: the client's existing copy and brand.
- Typography: the existing pack. A change to type is a brief change, not a revision.
- Pacing: preserve; adjust only the scenes the revision names.
- Hook: unchanged unless the revision names it.
- Highlight: the requested changes, each traceable to a line in the brief.
- Landing: unchanged unless the revision names it.
- Transitions: unchanged.

For a revision the plan gate is a diff: list each requested change, the scene it
touches, and the reading-time result before and after.
